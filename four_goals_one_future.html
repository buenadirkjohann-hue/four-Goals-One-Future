Our Four Sustainable Development Goals

These four goals are connected because the choices we make about water and resources affect our health, while our environmental actions help protect the future.

SDG 3

Good Health and Well-Being

Promote healthy lives and support physical and mental well-being for everyone.

SDG 6

Clean Water and Sanitation

Ensure access to safe water, sanitation, and good hygiene.

SDG 12

Responsible Consumption and Production

Use resources wisely and reduce waste and unnecessary consumption.

SDG 13

Climate Action

Take action to reduce climate change and prepare communities for its effects.

Problems and Solutions

SDG 3 • Health
Problem: Poor hygiene, unhealthy habits, pollution, and limited access to health information can increase health risks.
Solution: Practice good hygiene, eat nutritious food, stay physically active, care for mental well-being, and support health education.

SDG 6 • Clean Water
Problem: Water pollution, unsafe drinking water, and poor sanitation can spread disease and damage communities.
Solution: Keep waterways clean, avoid dumping waste, conserve water, maintain proper sanitation, and protect clean water sources.

SDG 12 • Responsible Consumption
Problem: Excessive buying, single-use products, and improper waste disposal create pollution and waste resources.
Solution: Follow the 5Rs—Refuse, Reduce, Reuse, Repair, and Recycle. Choose durable products and dispose of waste properly.

SDG 13 • Climate Action
Problem: Greenhouse-gas emissions, deforestation, and excessive resource use contribute to climate change.
Solution: Save electricity and water, use cleaner transportation when possible, plant and protect trees, reduce waste, and support climate-friendly practices.

How the 4 Goals Connect

They work as a cycle. Responsible consumption reduces waste and pollution. Less pollution helps protect water and the climate. Clean water and a healthier environment reduce health risks. Healthy communities can then participate more effectively in protecting the environment.

What Can We Do?

💧 Save Water

Turn off faucets when not needed and report leaks.

♻️ Reduce Waste

Bring reusable bottles and containers and avoid unnecessary packaging.

🌱 Protect Nature

Plant trees, keep surroundings clean, and protect green spaces.

❤️ Stay Healthy

Practice hygiene, eat balanced meals, exercise safely, and care for your well-being.
